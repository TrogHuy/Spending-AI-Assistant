| Tổng phí, Miễn phí | Total fee, Free |
| Danh mục | Category |

## Categories (draft, **[Decision]**)

| Category | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| Food and drink | Meals, cafes, groceries, delivery |
| Transport | Ride-hailing, fuel, parking, tickets |
| Shopping | Goods and online orders |
| Bills and utilities | Electricity, water, internet, phone, rent paid to a known provider |
| Entertainment | Games, streaming, events |
| Health | Pharmacy, clinics |
| Education | Tuition, courses, books |
| Transfers to people | Person-to-person transfers, unlabeled or labeled by the user |
| Income | Money in |
| Other | Anything else |

## Risk terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Payee type | individual, business, or uncertain |
| Newness | new, known, or unknown (not enough history) |
| Risk flag | none, review, caution. Advisory only |
| One-tap ask | Single question on a flagged row ("Cho việc gì?") whose answer becomes a per-user rule |
| Pre-payment check | User enters payee, amount and purpose before paying and gets a verdict |
| Payee key | Identity used to merge payees: account + bank, phone, or name + bank |
